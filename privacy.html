Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Applies to Tezzie played on this website and to the Tezzie apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Summary: Tezzie does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

WHO WE ARE

Tezzie is published by SARJ Virtual Associates LLC ("we", "us"). You can reach us at Robertj516@gmail.com.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

None. The game has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and it does not send game information over the internet.

WHAT THE GAME SAVES ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Your high score, mission stars and best times.
  • Levels you build or import in the Level Editor.
  • Your sound setting, chosen paint color and whether your car photo is flipped.
  • If you choose to use one, a small pixelated copy of a car photo.

In the browser, this is kept in your browser's local storage for this website. In the apps, it is kept in the app's private storage. Either way it stays on your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browsing data or deleting the app erases it.

PHOTOS AND CAMERA

Tezzie opens a photo picker only when you choose "Load car photo" in the Garage. The picture you choose is shrunk on your device into a small game sprite. It is never uploaded or shared, and the game does not look at any other photos.

CHILDREN

Tezzie is made for all ages, including young children, and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

THIRD PARTIES

The game and this website contain no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and load nothing from other websites. Apple and Google may collect information about downloads from their stores under their own privacy policies.

THIS WEBSITE

This website does not use cookies. It may save a copy of the game in your browser so it loads faster and works offline. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on this page.
