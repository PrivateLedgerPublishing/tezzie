COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need to download anything?
No. Press PLAY FREE and the game runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Phone and tablet apps are coming soon.
Is it really free?
Yes. There are no ads, no purchases and no account to make.
How do I fly?
On a keyboard, the arrow keys turn, thrust and brake, and Space zaps. On a phone or tablet, use the arrow buttons on the left to turn; on the right, JET thrusts, BRK slows you down and FIRE zaps. Hold FIRE for automatic fire. Slow bumps bounce you off walls; fast hits cost you a try.
The game is tiny on my phone.
Turn your phone sideways. The game is built for a wide screen.
The keys don't do anything.
Click once on the game so it has your keyboard's attention, then try again.
Why can't I zap in the first levels?
Tezzie learns to fly first. Spark Zap arrives in the fifth Orbit Ring level, and the classic worlds add a new ship and weapon from World 2 on.
How do I get three stars?
One star for finishing a level, one for collecting every spark, and one for beating the par time.
How do I share a level I built?
Open the Level Editor, press SHARE and copy the code. Your friend opens Missions, then Import code, and pastes it.
A mission is too hard.
Crashes only cost time. Checkpoints save your spot, and you can restart any time from the pause menu.
There's no sound.
Check the SOUND button on the main menu or in the pause menu, then your device's volume and silent switch. Browsers only play sound after you tap or press a key.
My progress disappeared.
Progress is saved in your browser on this device only. Private or incognito windows, clearing your browsing data, or switching browsers or devices starts you fresh. Share codes for levels you want to keep.
Does Tezzie collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
